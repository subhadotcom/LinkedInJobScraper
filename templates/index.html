LinkedIn Job Scraper

This tool allows you to scrape job listings from LinkedIn based on keywords and location.

The scraping process may take some time. Please be patient.
Enter job keywords like "Python Developer" or "Marketing Manager"
Enter job location or leave blank for all locations
LinkedIn shows approximately 25 jobs per page

How It Works

  1. Enter your job search keywords (required) and location (optional).
  2. Select the number of pages to scrape (more pages = more results but slower).
  3. Click "Start Scraping" to begin the process.
  4. The tool will collect job listings from LinkedIn based on your criteria.
  5. View the results and export them to CSV or JSON format.
This tool is for educational purposes only. Please respect LinkedIn's terms of service and use responsibly.
